- What work was delegated to AI?

### 3. Discernment

- How was the AI output verified? Examples: test, diff, screenshot, log, human confirmation.
- Did anyone rely only on AI self-report, polished summary, or incomplete evidence?

### 4. Diligence

- Were there privacy, secret, internal-context, public-package, over-permission, or accidental-commit risks?
- Did the work follow minimum disclosure and public/private boundary rules?
